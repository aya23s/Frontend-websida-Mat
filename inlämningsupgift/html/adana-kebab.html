Adana Kebab

Adana kebab is a famous dish made from finely minced lamb, mixed with red pepper, chili, and spices, then shaped around a wide metal skewer and grilled over charcoal. Originating from the city of Adana in southern Turkey, it is known for its smoky flavor, tender texture, and spicy kick. It is traditionally served with flatbread, grilled vegetables, fresh herbs, and sumac onions.

History

Kebabs have a long history throughout the Middle East, where grilling meat over open flames has been a culinary tradition for centuries. Different regions developed their own variations, using local spices, ingredients, and cooking techniques. Adana kebab emerged as a distinctive specialty of Adana, Turkey, reflecting the region’s rich food culture and grilling traditions. Today, it is one of the most iconic dishes in Turkish cuisine and part of the broader Middle Eastern kebab tradition, enjoyed across the region and around the world.

Adana Kebab Ingredients:

  • 800 g ground lamb (preferably with some fat)
  • 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped or minced
  • 1–2 red chili peppers, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes (pul biber)
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp ground cumin (optional)
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped parsley (optional)

For Serving:

  • Middle eastern flatbread or lavash
  • Grilled tomatoes and green peppers
  • Sliced red onions with sumac
  • Fresh parsley
  • Lemon wedges
