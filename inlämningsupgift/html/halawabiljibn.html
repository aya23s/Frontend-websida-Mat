Halawa bil jibn

Halawa bil Jibn is a traditional Lebanese and Levantine dessert made from a soft, stretchy dough of cheese and semolina, filled with sweet cream known as ashta. The rolls are cut into bite-sized portions and topped with crushed pistachios and fragrant sugar syrup, often flavored with orange blossom water or rose water. It is loved for its delicate texture, creamy filling, and perfectly balanced sweetness.

History

Halawa bil Jibn has deep roots in the culinary traditions of the Levant, particularly in Lebanon and Syria. The dessert reflects the region’s longstanding tradition of combining cheese, semolina, cream, nuts, and aromatic syrups to create distinctive sweets. It is especially associated with Syrian cities such as Hama and Homs and has become a favorite throughout Lebanon and the wider Middle East. Today, it is commonly served at celebrations, family gatherings, and special occasions, remaining a cherished part of Lebanese and Levantine dessert culture.

Ingredients for dough:

  • 500 g unsalted mozzarella cheese
  • ¾ cup fine semolina
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2–4 tbsp water, if needed

For the ashta cream filling:

  • 500 ml whole milk
  • 200 ml heavy cream
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp orange blossom water (optional)

For the sugar syrup:

  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup water
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp orange blossom water or rose water (optional)
